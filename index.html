About

Media and TMT are industries with hard-to-read data — fast-moving markets, opaque revenue, and decisions that need both quantitative rigor and real industry knowledge.

That's the work I do. I'm an applied economist finishing my M.S. at the University of Cincinnati, working across financial analysis, econometrics, and media markets. I've analyzed $500M+ in cross-border ownership and investment records on Bloomberg Terminal, applied time-series and panel econometrics to media revenue and policy, and researched how EU regulation is reshaping digital platforms and TMT markets.

I also know the media business from the operating side. Five years of consulting — directing budgeting, forecasting, and strategy for 20+ media projects across 14 countries — means I understand how media companies actually make and spend money, not just how to model it.

The combination is the point: financial and quantitative skills, plus genuine domain knowledge of media and TMT.

Media & Entertainment · Private Credit
Crescent Harbor Media Holdings — $75M senior secured facility
Investment Committee underwriting · library-backed borrowing base

I underwrote a $75.0M first-lien unitranche facility secured against a film and TV library, tax credits, and international pre-sales — the kind of structured deal that sits squarely between media and finance.

The work runs the full stack: a discounted-cash-flow library valuation, borrowing-base mechanics with advance rates and haircuts, a debt-service schedule and covenant package, plus sensitivity, scenario, and recovery analysis. The recommendation — Approve with Conditions — turns on downside borrowing-base coverage of 0.97x and a modeled maturity refinancing gap, not headline pricing.
